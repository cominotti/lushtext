# Product Guidelines

## Documentation Prose Style
- Use **Technical and Precise** language for all documentation.
- Prioritize technical accuracy, clarity, and depth for developer and user guides.

## UI/UX Principles
- **Adwaita Native:** Strictly follow the GNOME Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) and utilize native Libadwaita widgets.
- **Power User Efficiency:** Prioritize keyboard shortcuts, fast interactions, and optimized workflows for experienced users.
- **Minimalist Focus:** Maintain an uncluttered and focused editing environment, minimizing distractions.

## Visual Design
- **Standard GNOME:** Employ standard Adwaita styles with high-contrast native widgets and familiar GNOME aesthetics.

## Voice and Tone
- **Helpful and Polite:** All user-facing strings and communication should be empathetic, helpful, and polite.
