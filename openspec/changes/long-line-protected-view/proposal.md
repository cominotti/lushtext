## Why

LushText must stay instantly responsive (open, scroll, every interaction) with gigantic files, and it must never change a file's bytes. C1 (`document-scale-policy`, PR #59) profiles every body's paragraphs on a worker, marks a document `protected_view` when its longest paragraph exceeds `L_PROTECT` (500, C0's idle-confirmed stall basis) or a window of `R_BATCH` (224) paragraphs exceeds `V_BATCH` (56,000) characters (this change re-keys that flag, below), and turns off syntax, minimap, invisibles and search highlighting above their own thresholds. What C1 cannot remove is GTK's: GtkTextView lays a paragraph out whole over its visible text (A23: 34–42 ms per 200,000 characters unwrapped, 177–217 ms wrapped; Pango overflows `int32` past 238,319 characters, A28), and GtkTextBuffer pays whole-paragraph costs that hidden text does not reduce (log attrs, A26, about 95 ms per call on the 1.9M-character reference line; IM surrounding text, A30; AT-SPI text, A35; the in-tab `GtkSourceSearchContext`, 25–28 s). The maintainer decision of 2026-10-11 resolves this with **Protected View**: documents C1 marks open view-only, the buffer is never changed, giant paragraphs are folded for display only, and every path that would place a caret, select, edit or search inside them is replaced by a bounded LushText-owned path. Editing them is deferred to an optional C5.

A new finding makes this change more urgent (2026-10-29): the crash-recovery smoke fails on a slow CI runner with `recovery-restore-complete blocked by draft-autosave`. Recovering its two 32 MiB single-line drafts is a CPU-bound main-thread stall of 31–53 s in the debug build at 0.55–2 CPUs: the layout of a paragraph of about 33.5 million visible characters. Folding at insertion removes it; this change owns it (design D13).

This revision (2026-10-29) re-bases the 2026-10-11 proposal on C1's implemented API, C0's idle-confirmed values and the frame-correct lane, and records an adversarial review (design, "Adversarial review").

## What Changes

- **Protected View entry at a measured edit-stall threshold** (coordinator decision, 2026-10-30; the maintainer may override). C1's `DocumentScale::protected_view` is re-keyed to hold exactly when the longest paragraph exceeds **`L_EDIT_STALL`**, the shortest paragraph length at which one keystroke inside it exceeds `B_interact` on the reference machine, measured under the quiet gate (7 repetitions) before implementation. `L_PROTECT` (500) stays a C1 profile threshold and no longer makes anything view-only; density no longer enters Protected View. Below `L_EDIT_STALL` documents stay editable under C1's reduced features, so prose, notes, JSON Lines and this repository's `AGENTS.md` stay editable. C0's `L_protect_stall` (500 / 710) measured a similar quantity; if `L_EDIT_STALL` lands near it, the choice returns to the maintainer (design D0). Entry happens at load, eviction reload, external reload, reopen with encoding, draft recovery, set-aside open, local-history restore and Undo Restore (from the body profile C1 attaches with `BufferReplacementRequest::with_profile`), and from C1's edit-time watch. It is monotone for the editor session, with two exits: a reload from disk re-derives it, and **one bounded undo of the edit that caused entry**, offered by the alert and Ctrl+Z, restores the document and its previous feature policy.
- **Buffer immutability.** While protected, no operation modifies the buffer. A document installed protected stays unmodified and triggers no draft, autosave or local-history capture. A recovered or edit-growth document stays modified, and its saves and drafts keep working.
- **Display-only fold at insertion, by a windowed rule.** Every paragraph shows at most `head_cap = min(N_VISIBLE, N_HEAD, A28_SAFE_CHARS)` characters, where `N_HEAD` is newly measured (the largest head whose first layout and wrap-toggle re-layout fit `B_settle`; `N_VISIBLE` is a scroll-only threshold). A paragraph that lies in a window of `R_fold` paragraphs whose capped sum exceeds `V_BATCH` shows at most `N_DENSE` = 250. Nothing else is folded. Heads are never hidden. The tail is inserted already carrying one `invisible=true, editable=false` tag. A pure, streaming policy in `model/protected_fold.rs` (Kani-proved) computes the plan in the worker pass that runs C1's `ParagraphProfileAccumulator`, and one cross-cutting GTK executor applies it for every whole-content install. The first draft's excess budget was replaced: with C0's values it folded every paragraph over 250 characters in every protected document.
- **One immutable source, retained from the install.** Find, the Line Inspector, whole-line copy and every save of a protected document read `ProtectedTextSource`, which is the install's own body moved into long-lived, charged ownership. No GTK snapshot of a protected buffer runs, because the chunked snapshot is quadratic inside a single-segment paragraph (new cost axiom P8). Only an edit-growth entry captures, once.
- **Caret-free, edit-free interaction.** `editable=false` and `cursor-visible=false` set before the first install byte (A37); a `move-cursor` intercept (caret browsing included); a capture-phase gesture claiming presses and drags (touch pans stay unclaimed); DnD off; pre-emption of every GtkTextView and GtkSourceView binding that can move a mark or change the buffer (including `select-all`, F7, `menu.popup`, `change-number`, `change-case`, `join-lines`, `move-to-matching-bracket`), checked by a class-shortcut enumeration test; a Protected View context menu; one bounded notice. On edit-growth entry the marks are placed at the grown paragraph's start in the entry emission.
- **Line Inspector**, **navigation by a `protected-nav` mark**, **worker-side in-tab find** (searches hidden text too, A33), **print refused**, **minimap off** for the session, **invisibles off** while a fold exists, and **syntax following C1** (`L_SYNTAX` 2,000 is above `L_PROTECT`, so short protected documents keep highlighting).
- **Saves.** Every save of a protected document writes the source on the worker with the recorded encoding and BOM, no transformations and no mirror-back; `Mixed` line endings are written verbatim; a decode that cannot round-trip refuses Save As of an unmodified document.
- **Draft recovery of giant paragraphs (new).** Recovery installs through the fold executor, enters Protected View modified, retains the body as the source, and writes no autosave snapshot. The crash-recovery smoke must pass within its existing 60 s limit; the remaining one-turn hidden-tail insert is recorded as the input to C3's adoption gate.
- **Automation.** Tab snapshots gain `protected_view`, `protected_view_reason`, `entry_undo_available`, `folded_paragraph_count` and `hidden_character_count`; the surface snapshot gains `line_inspector_visible`. Protected find holds `editor-search`; fold application stays inside `file-load`; recovery inside `draft-autosave`.
- **Workflow convention.** A `WFR-PROTECTED-VIEW` row with its role home at `ui/editor_page/protected_view/`.
- **GTK axiom ledger.** P1–P8, numbered after the last applied entry (A51 once C4c lands): capture-phase claims, non-editable IM, the `move-cursor` intercept, AT text and AT caret writes on a folded paragraph (lane-pinned, extended to word, sentence and line granularity), DnD, key-binding pre-emption, `insert_with_tags` of a hidden run (structure and cost), and the snapshot-depth cost.
- **Verdict under the quiet gate.** The budget verdict is measured with the frame-correct lane under the coordinator's reference precondition (quiet gate before, in and after the measurement lock, a sampler during the run), 7 repetitions, the three lane variants, and the four construction profiles for cases that start a process, against main's binary.
- **Docs.** Accessibility guide, matrix and Orca checklist entry, automation docs, end-user coverage, workflow matrix, README, `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/rules/ui.md`, `docs/next/long-line-documents.md`, `docs/next/long-line-evolution.md` and `docs/next/formal-verification.md`.
- **Dependency.** C0 (archived) and C1 (PR #59, must be on `main` first). This change edits C1's `DocumentScale::for_profile` (the `protected_view` key) and the session join (the entry-undo restore).
- **C3.** `sliced-protected-install`'s adoption gate now names the 32 MiB draft-recovery case.
- **BREAKING (user-visible):** documents whose longest paragraph exceeds the measured `L_EDIT_STALL` are no longer editable. Today they are editable but stall on every keystroke.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. The Protected View requirements extend `long-line-document-presentation`, which C1 introduces.

### Modified Capabilities

- `long-line-document-presentation`: Protected View entry, monotonicity and the entry undo, buffer immutability, the windowed display-only fold at insertion, caret-free interaction, the Line Inspector, navigation into hidden tails, worker-side find, print, secondary views, the fold marker, and the Kani requirement (ADDED); and C1's requirements "One monotone document scale policy governs feature availability" (`protected_view` re-keyed to `L_edit_stall`) and "Edits downgrade features synchronously and never re-enable them" (the entry-undo exception) (MODIFIED, every scenario name kept).
- `main-thread-responsiveness`: Protected View interactions and settles within `B_interact` and `B_settle`, the declared one-turn hidden-tail envelope, the source rule (no GTK snapshot of a protected buffer), and the quiet-gate verdict.
- `document-save-safety`: every protected save writes the retained source verbatim; Save As byte identity; non-round-trippable decodes refused.
- `draft-session-recovery`: caret-free session restore, and recovery of giant-paragraph drafts through the fold executor with no autosave snapshot.
- `editor-inline-alerts`: the Protected View notice replaces C1's reduced-features alert for the same document.
- `dbus-automation-spine`: snapshot fields, readiness reuse.
- `search-replace-safety`: in-tab Replace unavailable; no search context attached.
- `gtk-axiom-ledger`: P1–P8.
- `gtk-accessibility-spine`: the folded main view, the Line Inspector as the accessible path, the AT fallback, the Orca entry.
- `workflow-readability-boundaries`: the `WFR-PROTECTED-VIEW` row.
- `invisible-character-visualization`: invisibles forced off while a fold exists.
- `local-history`: no capture for installed-protected documents; the protected, caret-free preview.
- `live-editor-memory-budget`: the retained source is charged and released with eviction.
- `external-file-monitor-coverage`: external reload re-folds at insertion and replaces the source.
- `line-bookmarks`: bookmark navigation scrolls without a caret; toggle is unavailable.
